Put 2-5 royalty-free MP3 background music tracks in this folder (upbeat,
energetic instrumental works best for this genre). The bot picks one at
random and plays it quietly under the narration.

Where to get genuinely royalty-free tracks:
- YouTube Audio Library (studio.youtube.com -> Audio Library)
- Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music)
- Free Music Archive (freemusicarchive.org) — filter by CC0 / no-attribution

If this folder is empty, the bot just uses narration only — nothing breaks.
